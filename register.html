CHOOSE YOUR ARENA.TWO WAYS TO COMPETE

NEXORA TechSprint 2K26 runs in two editions. One is played in person on the S-VYASA campus, the other entirely online. They are open to different students — read the eligibility line on each card before you pick, because your college location decides which one you can enter.

◆ Offline · On campus

THE NEXORAOffline championship

The flagship two-day championship, played in person at S-VYASA Deemed to be University Bengaluru Extension Campus Sattva Global City. Five challenges, real judges, one national stage — you build, debug and present on campus with your team.

Who can enter Only students currently studying at a college in Karnataka. Bring your college ID — it is checked at the venue.
  • Two days on campus, five rounds
  • Team pass ₹499 for a team of four
  • Kits, meals and certificates for every member

◆ Online · From anywhere

NEXORA WEBSPACEOnline championship

The online edition. You compete remotely, from your own desk, in a timed online arena — same challenge spirit, no travel needed. Everything runs through the registration form and the link we send your team.

Who can enter Only students currently studying at a college outside Karnataka. Your college must be in another state or union territory of India.
⚠ Not open to students studying in Karnataka. If your college is in Karnataka, you cannot take part in NEXORA WEBSPACE — please register for THE NEXORA instead.
  • Played fully online, from your own campus or home
  • Timed online rounds, judged remotely
  • Certificates and rewards for the winning teams
